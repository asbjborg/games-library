## Original mockup briefs

The first brief requested one 3×3 contact sheet. Each tile featured the same single game.
The directions were Cinematic, Gallery, Field Manual, Bold Type, Playroom, Precision, Journal, Terminal, and Observatory.
The cinematic direction used a charcoal world, amber energy routes, cyan crystals, large white typography, and an orange play button.

The second brief expanded Cinematic into a three-column, two-row library.
It featured Lattice, Undertow, Night Shift, Mossbound, Driftline, and Dead Air.
Only Lattice was marked playable. The other entries were clearly marked Concept.

These are summaries of the mockup briefs. The production artwork prompt below is preserved verbatim.

## Production artwork prompt

Reference: `01-design-directions.png`, top-left tile only.
Mode: built-in imagegen, opaque background.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production website hero background illustration.
Use ONLY the top-left "01 CINEMATIC" tile of the attached contact sheet as the visual reference. Create a full-bleed standalone cinematic landscape illustration for the browser strategy game Lattice. Remove all website UI, logos, labels, borders, typography, and buttons. NO TEXT anywhere.
A dramatic mountainous world built on subtle hexagonal terrain. Forested rocky islands, narrow water channels, elegant amber power towers joined by luminous golden energy routes, clusters of luminous cyan mineral crystals. A tall central energy tower slightly right of center, other smaller towers farther into the valley, misty layered mountain peaks, warm dusk breaking through slate-blue storm clouds. The world should feel intricate, tangible and mysterious. Preserve the gorgeous high-end game key-art feel of the reference, realistic miniature strategy world, exquisite rocks and trees, carefully designed fine towers and connectors, warm glowing energy and cold terrain. No people, no fantasy creatures, no armies. The amber network is the visual focus.
Wide landscape 16:9, as large and crisp as possible. Place the main spectacle across center and right two thirds. Lower left should be naturally dark rocky terrain with calmer detail so white HTML title and description remain readable later. Upper third atmospheric mountains and sky, no artificial black rectangle. Rich full natural scene, restrained volumetric mist and warm light shafts. This is aspirational cover illustration, not a gameplay screenshot. No HUD. Match the reference world and palette closely.
```
