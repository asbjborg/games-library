# Future game concepts

Created during the Games Library design session on 4 October 2026.
Teddy requested that these ideas remain in the repository. The public site features only Lattice.

These are creative starting points, not projects in development or promised releases.
Names are working titles. The proposed first versions below are design suggestions.

## Lattice — the starting point

**Real-time strategy · Playable**

> Build your network. Hold the line.

Connect an energy network across a hexagonal world. Mine resources, expand territory,
and maintain the network while enemy waves attack.

Previously named Hex RTS. The game remains in the separate `lattice` repository.

## Undertow

**Submarine survival · Concept**

> Keep the lights on. Stay below.

Explore the deep in a small, ageing submarine. Balance oxygen, power, and pressure.
Decide whether that shape on sonar is worth investigating.

- **Core loop:** descend, scan, investigate, repair, and return with enough resources for another dive.
- **Interesting choice:** divert power to propulsion, life support, lights, or sonar. You cannot run everything at full strength.
- **Atmosphere:** cold teal darkness, warm cabin lights, creaking metal, and distant shapes beyond the searchlights.
- **First playable slice:** one submarine, a compact depth map, power allocation, one leak, and one salvage objective.

## Night Shift

**Systems management · Concept**

> One technician. Too many alarms.

Keep a server room alive through the night. Route power, manage cooling, and respond to failures before they spread.
Play the situation straight. Let the systems create the absurdity.

- **Core loop:** inspect alerts, find the actual fault, redirect capacity, repair equipment, and survive the next incident.
- **Interesting choice:** restore service quickly or fix the root cause while the queue keeps growing.
- **Atmosphere:** quiet fans, cold rack lights, orange alarms, tangled cables, and a coffee that never stays warm.
- **First playable slice:** one room, six racks, power and heat systems, and a short sequence of interacting faults.

The appeal is learning the room's dependencies. Random alarms alone would become a clicking chore.

## Mossbound

**Ecosystem puzzler · Concept**

> Bring a forgotten world back to life.

Restore an abandoned landscape through water, sunlight, and plants.
Solve ecological puzzles as roots reclaim old machines and greenery reconnects isolated patches.

- **Core loop:** observe a damaged area, redirect water, introduce plants, and watch the habitat change.
- **Interesting choice:** one species creates shade or moisture that helps another, but blocks a third.
- **Atmosphere:** emerald moss, small streams, dark stone, soft sunlight, and quiet mechanical remnants.
- **First playable slice:** one diorama, three plant species, a water route, and a restoration objective.

## Driftline

**Orbital salvage · Concept**

> Catch the wreckage. Build a way home.

Pilot a small salvage tug through an abandoned orbital field.
Catch useful wreckage, build up the ship, and manage momentum with limited fuel.

- **Core loop:** identify salvage, match velocity, attach a tow line, and recover the piece safely.
- **Interesting choice:** valuable debris adds mass and changes handling. A heavy prize can leave you stranded.
- **Atmosphere:** quiet industrial space, a huge broken orbital ring, dusty planets, and warm engine flares.
- **First playable slice:** a two-dimensional debris field, thrusters, one tether, a fuel budget, and a recovery zone.

## Dead Air

**Signal mystery · Concept**

> Someone is still transmitting.

Work a shortwave radio in a remote lookout. Find strange broadcasts and use their clues to locate the source.
The mystery unfolds through listening, tuning, and deciding which signals deserve attention.

- **Core loop:** scan frequencies, isolate a signal, decode its message, and compare it with the map and previous broadcasts.
- **Interesting choice:** signals overlap or appear briefly. Following one can mean missing another.
- **Atmosphere:** amber radio dials, stormy pine forests, a distant red beacon, and voices barely above the static.
- **First playable slice:** one radio, three frequencies, a small map, and a complete ten-minute mystery.

## Shared direction

Build compact games with strong systems and a distinct atmosphere.
Choose one satisfying loop before adding scale. Artwork can establish the mood without promising identical in-game rendering.
